PORT MERROW HARBOUR TIDE GAUGE - ANNUAL DATA SUMMARY
Tidewater Resilience Lab. Data processed by Samuel Achebe.

Record length: 1952 to present (72 years), gaps in 1963 and 1977.
Long-term trend in mean sea level: +2.9 mm per year.
Trend since 1993: +4.1 mm per year.
Total rise since 1952: about 21 cm.

Highest recorded water levels (above mean high water springs):
1. 1.34 m - the great storm of 1987
2. 1.18 m - winter 2014
3. 1.11 m - 1953
4. 1.02 m - autumn 2020
5. 0.97 m - 1990

Number of days per year with water above the lowest section of the harbour wall:
1960s: 0.3 days per year
1990s: 1.1 days per year
2010s: 3.4 days per year

Note: the rise in overtopping days is driven by both higher mean sea level and settlement of the wall. See the flood risk baseline (TRL-2024-03).
