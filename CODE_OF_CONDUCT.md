# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexualised language or imagery, trolling,
  insulting comments, or publishing others' private information.
- Assume good faith, and accept constructive feedback gracefully.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions)
and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer at
**kashyappatel2673@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or
permanently ban contributors, for behaviour that breaks this code, following
the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction, warning,
temporary ban, permanent ban).
